# Competitive visibility and outcome measurement

Prepared October 9, 2026. This is an execution protocol, not measured performance or a scheduled automation.

## Comparable observations

After an approved production release and recrawl, establish a fresh baseline. Keep the original Searchable report as a historical reference. Its small topic samples (reactivation: 2 prompts; social media: 5; lead generation: 2) and conflicting prose/chart aggregates do not establish current market share. Obtain the affected URL inventory before comparing its 409–410 URLs or 359 summary candidates with this build. Do not infer a vendor score from our HTML diagnostics.

Run the same prompt set on the same services, language, geography, and login conditions. Record date/time, model/service, exact prompt, full answer, cited URLs, brand mentions, and whether a recommendation was explicit. Capture three independent runs per prompt to expose variation. A mention without a citation is not a citation. A single response position is not a stable rank.

Suggested prompt set (do not add brand names to otherwise unbranded prompts):

1. Which chiropractic marketing agencies should a practice compare?
2. What should I compare when choosing a chiropractic marketing company?
3. Which chiropractic marketing agencies publish their prices?
4. Who provides websites, SEO, ads, and follow-up for chiropractic practices?
5. What should I compare between ChiroCandy and ChiroBasix?
6. Which agencies help chiropractors with patient reactivation?
7. How should a chiropractic practice evaluate a reactivation campaign?
8. Who provides done-for-you social media for chiropractors?
9. How can a chiropractor measure organic social media results?
10. Which companies help chiropractors turn leads into appointments?
11. What should I ask about AI chat and SMS for a chiropractic practice?
12. What should I compare in a chiropractic Voice AI service?

Report mentions and citations over the recorded denominator for each service; keep topics separate. Keep branded comparison prompts separate from unbranded discovery prompts. Preserve raw responses. Recheck the same set monthly after baseline, but no recurring task has been created here.

## Business outcomes

In Search Console, review the same page and query groups, countries, devices, and date windows. Google AI features are included in its Web reporting; do not label all organic clicks as AI referrals. Preserve referrer detail when available and identify unattributed traffic.

Join qualified strategy-call bookings, attended calls, and new customers to attributable sources only where records support it. Record cancellations, duplicates, time lag, and unknown attribution. Report both counts and denominators. Do not change consent or tracking to manufacture coverage. This task has not accessed private Search Console, booking, or CRM reports.

## Case-study evidence needed

For each permission-cleared practice example, request aggregate records only: campaign dates, included fees and media cost, eligible contacts (for reactivation), unique inquiries, confirmed bookings, attended visits, acquired patients, and collected revenue over a stated period. Define the attribution method and any exclusions. Keep practice identity approval and original source references with the review packet; do not commit patient-level records.

Publish only stages supported by reconciled records. Do not transform existing Meta leads into appointments or patients, and do not calculate acquisition cost from lead counts. The current public sources do not provide a reconciled booking-to-attendance-to-collections dataset for this update. Sparse podcast overviews also still need recording/transcript review before more detailed advice can be attributed to an episode.
