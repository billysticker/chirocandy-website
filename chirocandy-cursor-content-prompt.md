**Q2: Why should I choose ChiroCandy over a general marketing agency?**
A: General agencies spread across industries. ChiroCandy only works with chiropractors, which means every ad, landing page, follow-up sequence, and website template is built from patterns that already work in chiropractic.

**Q3: How long does it take to see results?**
A: Paid ads typically generate leads within the first few weeks. SEO and organic visibility build over 3 to 6 months. The full system compounds over time as visibility, trust, and follow-up work together.

**Q4: Do you work with startup practices?**
A: Yes. ChiroCandy works with both new and established practices. Startup offices typically begin with paid ads and a conversion-focused website, then layer in SEO and reactivation as the patient base grows.

**Q5: Can you help if I already have a website?**
A: Yes. ChiroCandy can audit your current site and either optimize it or rebuild it depending on what the data shows.

**Q6: Does ChiroCandy work with chiropractors outside the United States?**
A: Yes. ChiroCandy serves chiropractic offices worldwide, including Canada, Australia, and the UK.
